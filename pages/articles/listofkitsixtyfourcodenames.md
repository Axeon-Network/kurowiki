This article shows a list of codenames used by [KitSixtyFour](KitSixtyFour) in their projects.

<h1 id="A">A</h1>
<hr>

<h2 id="Arctic+1">Arctic+1</h2>

Codename for a rewrite of [the original *Arctic*](KitSixtyFour_Arctic) with 32-bit support, as seen in *[Arctic32](Arctic32)*.

<h2 id="Atheros">Atheros</h2>

Preliminary name for *KitSixtyFour Arctic*.

<h2 id="Atsura">Atsura</h2>

Cancelled Minecraft server that would've been a merger of *[Horicraft](Horicraft)* and *[Horicraft (2)](Horicraft_(2))*.

<h1 id="B">B</h1>
<hr>

<h2 id="BetterLateThanNever">Better Late than Never</h2>

*[StuffyXP Website](StuffyXP_Website)* Update 2.1. Referencing that the update was delayed multiple times because of Kit [not having a PC to work on the update](TheCeleryPC#death-of-the-psu).

<h1 id="C">C</h1>
<hr>

<h2 id="Cuber">Cuber</h2>

An internal successor to *[HoriWebsite](HoriWebsite)*, which features a *Watercolor*-like interface, like the visual style present in [Windows Whistler build 2419](https://betawiki.net/wiki/Windows_XP_build_2419)

<h1 id="D">D</h1>
<hr>

<h2 id="DogUI">DogUI</h2>

User interface component planned for use in *Arctic* and *Arctic32*, later scrapped. It is a direct reference to [Dogui](Dogui).

<h2 id="Deltari">Deltari</h2>

The internal codename of the *[KuroWiki](KuroWiki)* Engine.

<h1 id="E">E</h1>
<hr>

<h2 id="EverybodyFeaturesUpdate">Everybody Features! Update</h2>

A cancelled update to *[Stuffy's Website](Stuffy's_Website]* with the former version number of 1.2.

<h1 id="H">H</h1>
<hr>

<h2 id="HappyNewYear!">Happy New Year!</h2>

*StuffyXP Website* Update 2.1.1.

<hr>

<h2 id="NewCoatOfPaint">New Coat of Paint</h2>

*StuffyXP Website* Update 2.0.[^1]

<h2 id="NishiBlade">Nishi Blade</h2>

Cancelled website planned to be a sucessor to *HoriWebsite*. The name is a merge of [Nishi](Nishi) (Kit's fursona) and *Blade*, the codename of the *Web Server* edition of [Windows Server 2003](https://betawiki.net/wiki/Windows_Server_2003).

<hr>

<h2 id="OneLastFix">One Last Fix</h2>

*StuffyXP Website* Update 2.1.2, a hotfix update made to address the *stuffyxp.github.io* to *horibyte.is-a.dev* migration bugs following *HoriWebsite*'s release.

<h2 id="ReKanto">ReKanto</h2>

The codename for the assembly rewrite of the [Nekori Arctic](Nekori_Arctic) kernel.

The assembly-based rewrite of the *Arctic* kernel, included in [public versions of Arctic](https://github.com/kitthebifox/arctic/releases).

<h2 id="Raybolt">Raybolt</h2>

A website that was planned to be a successor to HoriWebsite. It was later scrapped due to [being too ambitious](Raybolt#scrapped).

Cancelled successor to *HoriWebsite*, later scrapped due to being too ambitious. It is known that a Windows 2000-like UX was planned for this project.
