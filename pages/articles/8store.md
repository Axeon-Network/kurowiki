**The Return of the Windows Store in Windows 8.1?! (8Store)** is an [AstroNT](AstroNT) video made by [Avery](OneAveryAhead), primarily focused on reviewing the Windows 8.1 Store revival project app *8Store* but also around sideloading of Windows 8.1 "modern apps" (also known as Metro Apps) in general.

The video was initially planned as early as late December 2025, when Avery was one time scrolling through Reddit and happened to find a post about 8Store. The idea for such video was then briefly discussed on the private Discord server [AstroNT Advanced Server](AstroNT#AstroNT_Advanced_Server).

The debut of [TheHaswellLappy](TheHaswellLappy) on the AstroNT channel, recordings for what was considered by Avery "the first AstroNT video to use real hardware instead of virtual machines" generally took place around 3-5 February 2026, with video editing done on 12-14 February 2026.

It is the most popular AstroNT video with over 10,000+ views as of 1 March 2026, a number that especially increased since 22 February 2026 as the video was being pushed by YouTube's algorithm. Over 150+ subscribers of AstroNT's current 220+ have subscribed from the 8Store video.

22 February 2026 was also when it actually became the most popular video, overtaking the previous title holder, that of the first proper AstroNT video, *[Running VMware Workstation 12 on Windows Vista!](Running_VMware_Workstation_12_on_Windows_Vista)* by [Kit](KitSixtyFour) with 510+ views at the time.
