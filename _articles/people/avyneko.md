**AvyNeko** (name commonly stylized as **avyneko**),[^1] commonly known as **Avery** or **Ave** (born 17 August), is an European content creator, developer and (occasional) visual artist.

The founder and one of the managers of [Axeon Network](Axeon_Network) (including the Discord Server, [KuroWiki](KuroWiki) and [Orchid](Orchid)), they're also the creator of [several other projects](List_of_AvyNeko's_projects), and also makes content for the [AstroNT](AstroNT) channel. Their personal website is [AvySite](AvySite).

# Personal life
Avery was born on the date of 17 August in an unspecified place in Europe.[^2] Not much is known about Avery's personal life, as they chose not to disclose either due to privacy reasons or outright not being comfortable doing so, but it is known that they currently reside in Spain. Avery goes by *she/they* pronouns,[^3] and their timezone is currently Central European Time (UTC+1)[^4]. They currently label themselves as a *demigirl*.
