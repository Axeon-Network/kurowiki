**NovaLand**, officially known as the **Republic of NovaLand**, and initially known as the **Republic of Horiland** (and thus, commonly known as **Horiland**) was a country in a fictional version of Western Europe. It was bordered to the west by [Averia](Averia), to the east by [Dukan](Dukan), to the north by [???](), and to the south by the Atlantic Ocean. Its capital and largest city is [Fucking](Fucking,_NovaLand).

Canon-wise, the Republic of Horiland was founded on 2 May 1782 as a direct result of the *Europa Revolution War* (1770–1782) alongside other neighbour countries to eventually form what is now known as the modern-day (fictional) Europe. Previously, NovaLand was home to several indigenious cultures, and holds various historical places like the Starlight River, Deeto Ruins or the Castilian Museum.

After the English conquest of the South-Western European region in January of 1602, the country, alongside other states like Averia or Dunkan, were annexed to the brand-new *European-British Empire* (*The United European Empire of Great Britain*) in August of 1602. Prior to the conquest, the NovaLandese region (historically called *Siko* after Emperor Siko Hadi, Ruler of the Yusci) served as a major archeological site for the Yusco civilization.

Other former major urban areas include the cities of [Ture](Ture,_NovaLand) or [Jell](Jell,_NovaLand), which is colloqually described as "The City of Commerce". The currency of NovaLand was the [NovaLand Starbit](NovaLand_Starbit) (NSB.).

Citizens of NovaLand are called the NovaLandese. A populous country, NovaLand serves as the main industrial hub for companies such as [Dogui Heavy Industries Incorporated](Dogui_Heavy_Industries_Incorporated).

NovaLand is a democratic semi-presidential republic. As of May 2025, the current head of state (*President of the Republic of NovaLand*) is [KitSixtyFour](KitSixtyFour).
