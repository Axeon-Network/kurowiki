The ***Solarix Website*** was [AvyNeko](AvyNeko)'s personal website from 2017 to 2019.

# First iteration (May-Aug 2017)
The exact date when the first iteration of the Solarix Website (then known as Computerseasons.ml, and later as BriciWorld.ml during the lifecycle of this iteration) was released is unknown, but the earliest screenshots still in the Archives date back to May 2017.

Mostly barebones but with some content in it, the website was a simple tabbed layout made using the Material Design Lite CSS framework, attempting to take heavy inspiration from other niche websites that also used a MD 2014 style at the time.

The first website of the [BriciWorld Lounge](BriciWorld_Lounge) Discord Server from early August 2017 used this first iteration of BriciWorld.ml as its base.

This first iteration has been deprecated in late August 2017. It continued to be accessible until January 2018, when the source code for the website was deleted entirely.

# Second iteration (Aug-Oct 2017)
In late August 2017, the website has seen a complete revamp, a full-on improvement over the first iteration highlighting the evolution of AvyNeko's coding skills, while continuing to see the Material Design Lite CSS framework.

The website primarily just linked to social media profiles (located inside the drawer) and projects.

# Third iteration (Oct 2017-Jul 2018)
Just two months after the rollout of the second iteration, AvyNeko decided to try a different CSS framework that looked nicer (MDBootstrap), which led to a third iteration being rolled out in late October 2017, together with an announcement in the form of a blog post on the BriciWorld Blog. This iteration ended up being the longest in the life of the Solarix Website.

The homepage consisted of two rows of buttons linking to projects and socials respectively, with the navigation bar used to link to additional stuff. Additional About and Devices pages were later known to exist as of early 2018.

One of the main new "features" was the Dark Mode, however the implementation was very poor as it was actually a full duplicate of the same site but with the background and CSS assets changed to black, instead of using `prefers-color-scheme`. It was introduced and announced on the Lynxmic Blog in November 2017, a few weeks after the initial launch of the main third iteration website.

# Fourth iteration (Jul-Dec 2018)
In July 2018, the website has seen another fresh overhaul. This time it has been more simplified, fluently incorporating the then-new BW Tech branding, with the homepage looking like just a "landing page" with buttons to social media and other stuff. The About and Devices pages also continue to be present.

A short 1-minute [video](https://youtu.be/P3H8aoOx-LE) was posted to the [Lynxmic](Lynxmic) YT channel (then known simply as "BW Tech") on 8 July 2018 promoting the "fresh, clean" new website look, followed by an update in the same video where AvyNeko announced the start of YouTube streams after getting [a good enough computer](Amalthea).

Also in July 2018, a custom blog built from scratch was known to be worked on based on the fourth iteration of the website, also including a Gallery section (which later became a standalone page on the website itself). This was however abandoned after a short while due to complications, or rather a lack of webdev knowledge required to build such a blog. A similar idea would only be done in 2021 with the [Lynxmic Blog](Lynxmic_Blog), this time powered by the Jekyll static site generator.

No major updates have been released for this iteration aside from one in September 2018 to reflect the rebranding from BriciWorld/BW Tech to Solarix. It was ultimately replaced in January 2019 by the fifth website iteration, although it continued to be freely accessible through 2 April 2019, when the Solarix Website was discontinued as a whole.

# Fifth iteration (Jan-Apr 2019)
The fifth and final iteration of the website was initially made publicly available as a beta on 27 December 2018 and then fully replaced the fourth iteration on 9 January 2019. It received updates later on to be on track with the evolution of the solarixtech branding, but just like with the fourth iteration there haven't been any known major changes over time.

The website was finally discontinued on 2 April 2019, coinciding with the Lynxmic Twitch account deletion, due to AvyNeko finding a personal website for themselves "unnecessary" at the time.

Although this theory is not officially confirmed, it can be said that the discontinuation of Solarix Website was the beginning of a departure of AvyNeko from programming-related activities which culminated in the discontinuation of the [Venus](Venus) Discord Bot, the other major programming project by them at the time, in July 2019.
