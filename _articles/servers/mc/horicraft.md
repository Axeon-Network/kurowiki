**Horicraft**[^1] (formerly known as ***StuffyCraft***) was a creative Minecraft server owned and operated by [Kit](KitSixtyFour). It was initially announced by them on 3 December 2024, on the [StuffyXP Hangout](StuffyXP_Hangout) Discord Server, however it would only begin gaining significant popularity from late January 2025.

Main cities built in Horicraft include [Horilandese](Asteria) (now Asterian) cities like the capital [Phoenix](Phoenix) (formerly *Fucking*) (which was also the spawn area of Horicraft) or [Aether](Aether) (formerly *Jell*). As the world spawn, Phoenix is home to most of Horicraft's players as well as facitilies like [Dogui Heavy Industries Incorporated](Dogui_Heavy_Industries_Incorporated). Aether is mainly known for its tourist attraction, the *B Parkour*, shaped as a 🅱️ cube.

Players of Horicraft can travel between Aether, Phoenix, and other places using the [*Horicraft Underground Rail System*](Horicraft_Underground_Rail_System) (HURS, using minecarts), [*Horicraft Water Transportation System*](Horicraft_Underground_Rail_System#Horicraft_Water_Transportation_System) (HWTS, using boats), or using Nether Portals or pathes in cities by foot.

After just over 3 months of operation, Horicraft officially shut down on 11 March 2025 after a disagreement between the server hoster and Kit. Any instance of the original Horicraft that would remain open would no longer be official/endorsed by Kit. It was later succeeded by [Horicraft (2)](Horicraft_(2)), a survival Minecraft server.

In 18 May 2025, Horicraft was planned to merge with Horicraft (2) in order to make a """"new"""" server as an attempt to keep both alive. This was later scrapped in favor of [MidoriMC](MidoriMC) using Horicraft's world.
