***AstroNT Datacenter*** is a public Discord server owned by [KitSixtyFour](KitSixtyFour) and [AvyNeko](AvyNeko) serving as the official Discord community of the tech-oriented YouTube channel [AstroNT](AstroNT). It was created on 16 September 2026, exactly on the 1st anniversary of [MiloNXl](MiloNXl), a private Discord server that also used to be called AstroNT Datacenter, and opened a day later, on 17 September 2026, exactly on the 1st anniversary of the AstroNT channel itself.
