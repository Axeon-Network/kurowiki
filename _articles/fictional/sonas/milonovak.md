**Milo Dann Novak**[^1] (formerly known by the [mononym](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mononym) **Astro** prior to May 2026) is a fictional character created and designed by [KitSixtyFour](KitSixtyFour). He is an anthropomorphic dog who serves as the "official-unofficial"[^2] mascot for the [AstroNT Workstation](AstroNT) and [AstroNT Embedded](AstroNT_Embedded) YouTube channels, who are co-run by Kit alongside [AveryEclipse](AveryEclipse). Milo's middle and surname *Dann Novak* are references to [Dann Florek](en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dann_Florek) (an actor portraying [Donald Cragen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Cragen)) and [Casey Novak](en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casey_Novak) (a fictional character portrayed by [Diane Neal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diane_Neal)), both originating from *[Law & Order: Special Victims Unit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_&_Order:_Special_Victims_Unit)*.

First mentions of Milo date back to 16 May 2026, when a poll was published on AstroNT's community tab asking "if AstroNT was an animal, which one would it be?". The "Australian Shepherd" option won with 62% of respondents choosing it over the other three options: a Tuxedo Cat, an Orange Cat, and a Border Collie. After that, Milo's reference sheet was created by Kit three days later, officially depicting him as an Australian Shepherd. Although to this day, the official reference sheet has not been finalized due to time constraints, according to Kit, mainly due to "IRL things, alongside Astro[NT] and [Kuro[Wiki]](KuroWiki)".

Due to his breed's usual nature, Milo is active, loyal and playful. Ironically, he is not good at herding sheep.
