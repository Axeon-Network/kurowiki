**AstroNT**, formally **AstroNT Workstation** (known as *StuffyXP 2* prior to its public reveal) is a tech-focused YouTube channel ran and owned by [KitSixtyFour](KitSixtyFour) and [AvyNeko](AvyNeko) created on 17 September 2025 and launched on 25 October 2025. It is the official successor to [StuffyXP](StuffyXP) and [Lynxmic](Lynxmic).

As of 23 November 2026, the channel has over 1,600+ subscribers, 200,000+ views and a total of 30 videos. Content on the channel revolves primarily around operating systems and software, most of them being either experiments or exploration. The most popular video on the channel is *[The Return of the Windows Store in Windows 8.1?! (8Store)](The_Return_of_the_Windows_Store_in_Windows_8.1?!_(8Store))*, with over 40K+ views, with *[Windows 3.11... on the Modern Web?](Windows_3_11_on_the_Modern_Web)* in close proximity.

AstroNT has a sidekick channel known as [AstroNT Embedded](AstroNT_Embedded) which serves as an extras channel. External social media presence includes a Discord Server known as [AstroNT Datacenter](AstroNT_Datacenter), as well as a Bluesky account.
